## Decisions, risks or questions raised
- **Marking is per node, not per field.** A node's output is the state field the next nodes read, so the node is the natural unit; the mark is limited to agent and transform nodes, the two that fetch or compute values.
- **Short values are not scrubbed from other text.** Values under four characters stay out of checkpoints but are not replaced elsewhere, as replacing them would mangle unrelated text. Documented in the help.
- **The registry is thread-local**, the same choice `principals` and `trace_service` made.
